# Mood Mentor

## AI-Powered Employee Wellness Management Platform

Mood Mentor is an AI-powered Employee Wellness Management Platform designed to support workplace wellbeing through mood tracking, personalized wellness activities, wellness resources, interactive games, AI-powered guidance, wellness analytics, and employee feedback.

## Problem Statement

Employees often experience workplace stress, low motivation, reduced focus, and difficulty maintaining healthy work routines. Many existing wellness solutions lack personalization, continuous engagement, and accessible tools for monitoring wellbeing.

Mood Mentor addresses these challenges by providing employees with a single platform to track their mood, participate in wellness activities, access wellness resources, receive AI-guided support, and monitor their wellness progress.

## Objectives

- Enable simple and consistent mood tracking.
- Provide personalized wellness activities.
- Offer AI-powered conversational wellness support.
- Encourage mindfulness through books and interactive games.
- Provide analytics for understanding wellness progress.
- Collect employee feedback for continuous improvement.
- Promote healthier workplace habits.

## Key Features

### Mood Tracking

Employees can record their current mood and monitor their wellness progress over time.

### Wellness Activities

Users can participate in activities focused on relaxation, breathing, mindfulness, and healthy work routines.

### Wellness Books

The platform provides wellness-focused reading resources with an integrated reading experience and progress tracking.

### Wellness Games

Mood Mentor includes four interactive games:

- Memory Match
- Sudoku
- Number Rush
- Mindful Quiz

### AI Wellness Chatbot

The AI chatbot provides personalized wellness guidance based on the employee's current context and conversation.

### Wellness Analytics

Employees can view mood history, mood distribution, activity progress, wellness points, completed books and games, and feedback summaries.

### Employee Feedback

Employees can submit ratings and feedback to help evaluate and improve the wellness experience.
